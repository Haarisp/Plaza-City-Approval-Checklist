Restaurant Tenant Checklist — These are items you are responsible for obtaining and completing before your opening day. Submit applications early; city processing takes 2–8 weeks. Keep copies of all permits on-site at all times.
1
Business Registration & Licensing
Required
  • City Business LicenseDo First
    Register with the city clerk's office. Required before any other permits can be issued.
  • DBA / Assumed Name Filing (if applicable)
    File with the county if operating under a trade name different from your legal business name.
  • State Sales Tax Permit
    Apply through the state comptroller's office before collecting any sales tax.
  • Federal EIN (if hiring employees)
    Apply free at IRS.gov. Required for corporations, partnerships, and employers.
2
Health Department Approvals
Required
  • Food Service Establishment PermitDo First
    Apply with the city/county health department. Submit kitchen layout, equipment list, and food handling procedures for plan review.
  • Health Department Plan Review Scheduled
    Pre-opening review of kitchen design and sanitation setup. Schedule 2–4 weeks in advance.
  • Final Health Inspection — Passed
    On-site inspection of kitchen, refrigeration, handwashing stations, and food safety practices. Must pass before opening.
  • Food Manager Certification (ServSafe or equivalent)
    At least one certified food manager required on staff. Food handler cards may be required for all employees.
  • Dedicated Handwashing Stations Installed
    Separate from prep sinks. Must have hot/cold water, soap, and paper towels at all times.
3
Fire & Safety
Required
  • Hood & Fire Suppression System InstalledSchedule Early
    Required for open-flame or high-heat cooking. Licensed contractor only. Coordinate timing with building management (contact Miguel).
  • Fire Extinguishers Installed & Tagged
    Class K in kitchen; Class ABC in dining area. Must be inspected and tagged annually.
  • Emergency Exit Floor Plan Posted
    Visible evacuation map with all exits displayed inside the restaurant.
4
Buildout & Utility Permits
If Modifying Space
  • Building Permit for Tenant Improvements
    Required if modifying walls, plumbing, or electrical. All work by licensed contractors with management's written approval.
  • Blueprint / Floor Plan Submitted to City
    Show seating, kitchen, exits, bathrooms, and equipment placement. Required for CO and building permits.
  • Grease Trap Confirmed / InstalledIf Required
    Check with management and the city — required for most full kitchens; may apply to fruiterias. Must be inspected before opening.
  • Plumbing Permit & Inspection (if new plumbing)
    Required for any new sinks, drains, or water lines. Must be permitted and inspected by city.
5
Signage
Approval Before Install
  • Written Approval from ManagementDo First
    Submit sign design, size, and placement to management before applying for a city permit. Creativity is encouraged — make it memorable!
  • City Sign Permit Applied & Approved
    Submit dimensions, design, and placement plan to the city permitting office.
  • Sign Installed by Licensed Contractor
    No self-installation on exterior walls or above the unit entrance.
6
Outdoor / Cart Operations
If Applicable
  • Outdoor Vending / Cart Permit (City)Apply Early
    Required for corn carts or any sales outside your leased unit. Processing can take several weeks.
  • Food Truck / Mobile Unit Permit (if applicable)
    Separate permit required if operating both an indoor restaurant and an outdoor truck or cart.
  • Health Permit for Cart / Outdoor Unit
    Required even if connected to an already-permitted indoor kitchen.
7
Final Steps Before Opening
Required
  • All Permits Received & Posted On-Site
    Food service permit, CO, and business license must be displayed visibly inside your restaurant.
  • Insurance Certificate Submitted to Management
    General liability certificate naming the plaza as additional insured must be on file before opening.
  • Employee Records Ready (I-9, Food Handler Cards)
    Keep all employment eligibility forms and certifications on file and available for inspection.
  • Final Walkthrough with Plaza Management
    Schedule a walkthrough to confirm all plaza requirements are met before your open date.

🎉 All Done!

Every tenant item is checked. Congratulations and welcome to the plaza!

Building Owner / Management Checklist — These are tasks the plaza owner and management team must complete to prepare units for restaurant tenants and ensure the plaza meets all city requirements.
🤝
Coordination note: Some items require joint action between management and tenants — for example, hood installation requires management to schedule with Miguel and the tenant to hire a licensed contractor. Items that overlap with tenant responsibilities are noted.
1
Unit Readiness & Infrastructure
Owner Responsibility
  • Floor Plans Finalized & Sent to Each TenantDo First
    Send updated floor plan showing each unit's number, dimensions, and neighbors. Create per-unit versions as needed.
  • Water & Drainage Capacity Confirmed Per Unit
    Verify adequate supply and drainage for each tenant's business type — especially fruiterias and full kitchens.
  • Electrical Connections Mapped & Communicated to Tenants
    Notify tenants of connection locations via Aslam. Assign electrician for permitted work.
  • Hood Installation Scheduling Coordinated (Miguel)
    Facilitate scheduling with Miguel for all tenants installing hood systems. Arrange loans for qualifying existing tenants.
  • Equipment Move-In Window Communicated (May 15–End of May)
    Confirm move-in window with qualifying tenants. Clarify what items on walls can/cannot be relocated and document existing claims.
2
City & Permit Coordination
Owner Responsibility
  • TCO (Temporary Certificate of Occupancy) Status Tracked
    Monitor plaza-level TCO progress with the city and communicate milestone updates to tenants.
  • City Contacted re: Grease Trap Requirements
    Get first-hand city confirmation on which business types (including fruiterias) require a grease trap. Share findings with affected tenants.
  • Outdoor Cart / Corn Vendor Permit — City Inquiry MadeApply Early
    Contact city to understand timeline and requirements for outdoor cart permits on plaza property. Share with tenants requesting carts.
  • Joint / Expedited Inspection Program Explored
    Ask city about concurrent inspection scheduling or fast-track commercial permitting to reduce redundant visits across multiple restaurant tenants.
  • Blueprint Plan Requirement Clarified with City
    Confirm exactly what each restaurant tenant must submit in terms of blueprints before the city issues occupancy approval.
3
Tenant Communication & Documentation
Owner Responsibility

  • Lease Agreements Confirmed for All Tenants
    Verify signed leases and term commitments are on file. Confirm details such as Farooq's 1-year commitment.

Final Pre-Opening Walkthroughs
